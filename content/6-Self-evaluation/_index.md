My internship in the AWS First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) program, running from September 14, 2026 to December 6, 2026, brought me valuable experiences and fresh perspectives on cloud computing and information security. It was also a practical opportunity to apply what I learned at university to hands-on exercises and real-world tasks.

Throughout the program, I worked with a 5-member team to build **PhishGuard AI** – a serverless assistant for phishing alert triage and automated response steps. Alongside the project, I completed AWS labs, wrote technical guides, and joined four community events. These experiences helped me better understand how cloud services are deployed, how a project team coordinates, and how to document work so others can follow and use it effectively.

Looking back on the internship, I see steady progress in my technical knowledge, work ethic, and collaboration with others. At the same time, I recognize areas where I still need to grow, especially in presenting ideas, taking more initiative in technical discussions, and organizing shared tasks in a team.

### Needs Improvement

- **Clearer, more focused presentations:** Practice explaining ideas concisely and getting to the main point, especially when walking through technical topics.
- **More active networking and discussion:** Be more confident asking questions, sharing thoughts, and connecting with others at workshops, meetups, and community events.
- **Stronger task management:** Plan ahead, split tasks clearly, and track progress to coordinate better with the team and avoid last-minute crunches.
- **Closer collaboration when debugging:** Share blockers, compare what has already been tried, and troubleshoot together with teammates earlier instead of spending too long stuck alone.
- **Deeper analysis and trade-off evaluation:** Go beyond just making a system work by checking root causes, verifying outcomes, and weighing safety and efficiency across options.
- **More thorough testing and handover polish:** Pay closer attention to testing features after deployment and reviewing configurations and documentation before handover to reduce mistakes and lift quality.
- **Greater adaptability to new technologies:** Proactively explore new tools, services, and technologies to expand my skill set for changing project needs.

Through this internship, I have come to see technical knowledge as only one part of career growth; teamwork, communication, independent thinking, and a willingness to keep learning matter just as much. I will keep working on these areas, strengthen my core foundations, and build more hands-on experience for future projects and workplaces.
