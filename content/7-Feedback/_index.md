### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
What I felt most clearly when joining FCAJ was the opportunity to learn in an open environment where people can exchange knowledge, share experiences, and grow together. I also learned a lot through teamwork and connecting with people who share an interest in technology.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
I appreciate the accompaniment of the Organizers and Mentors throughout the program. Their guidance and shared experiences gave me more direction when approaching new knowledge. Through that, I also learned how to proactively explore, experiment, and discuss when facing difficulties.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
With my study orientation in Information Security, I see knowledge about AWS, Cloud, and AI as useful complementary content for my major. Approaching and practicing with new technologies also helped me broaden my perspective on how systems are built and operated in practice.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
Alongside technical knowledge, the program helped me practice teamwork, task coordination, building documentation, and presenting technical content. In particular, the PhishGuard AI project helped me better understand how to apply what I learned into a concrete product.

**5. Culture & Team Spirit**  
Workshops, meetups, and community events were memorable experiences for me. They were opportunities to listen to experiences from people in the industry, access new perspectives, and better understand practical requirements in the tech field.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
The program provides support and creates favorable conditions for interns during their work. The learning environment helps interns get exposed to practical work, learn from experience, and exchange with seniors and more experienced peers to improve knowledge and technical skills.


### Additional Questions
- What did you find most satisfying during your internship?
What satisfied me most was how the program combined learning theory and practice while creating opportunities for members to build a project together. The process of developing PhishGuard AI helped me gain more experience in teamwork, problem-solving, and approaching products from a practical angle.

- What do you think the company should improve for future interns?
Besides that, community activities also helped me learn many things beyond what is covered at university.
From a personal perspective, I hope the program has more in-depth discussions on practical topics such as architecture choices, AWS cost management, troubleshooting, and system security assessment.

- If recommending to a friend, would you suggest they intern here? Why or why not?
In addition, progress-sharing sessions or product reviews across teams would create opportunities for participants to see how others work and receive extra feedback to refine their projects.
I am willing to recommend FCAJ to friends interested in Cloud and AI, especially those who want to learn through hands-on practice. In my view, the value of the program lies not only in knowledge, but also in self-driven exploration, teamwork, and community connection.


### Suggestions & Expectations
- I hope FCAJ continues to maintain and expand connection activities among participants, Mentors, and the tech community. Practical experience-sharing sessions, project reviews, and topic-focused technical discussions will help participants understand more deeply how knowledge is applied in work.
- I hope FCAJ continues to grow so more students can experience the program. Personally, I also want to stay connected with the community, keep learning, and apply the knowledge I gained into upcoming projects.
- I would like to thank the Organizers, Mentors, and members who accompanied me throughout the program. FCAJ not only brought knowledge about AWS, Cloud, and AI, but also gave me valuable experiences in learning and teamwork.

I know I still have many things to improve, but I believe what I gained from the program will be useful preparation for my upcoming study and career journey.
